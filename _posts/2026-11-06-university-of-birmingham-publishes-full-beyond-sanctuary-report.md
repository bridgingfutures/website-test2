On **3 November 2026**, the University of Birmingham launched its full **28-page** report, *Beyond Sanctuary: Building the Future of Ukrainians in the UK*, in Parliament. It follows the [two-page policy brief published in June](https://bridgingfutures.uk/posts/beyond-sanctuary-building-the-future-of-ukrainians-in-the-uk/).

Drawing on a survey of **5,454 displaced Ukrainians**, **19 interviews with 20 practitioners**, and discussions with the Ukraine Policy Network, the report finds that many Ukrainians have built their lives in the UK, yet temporary immigration status continues to affect employment, education, wellbeing and long-term planning. It also examines the uncertainty faced by children and young people.

Two recommendations are particularly important for long-term immigration security:

* **Create a timely and affordable pathway to permanent residence** for displaced Ukrainians in the UK, including vulnerable groups.
* **Count lawful residence under the Ukraine Schemes and Ukraine Permission Extension (UPE)** towards existing settlement routes, including **Long Residence and Private Life**.

The full report provides a more detailed evidence base for moving beyond repeated temporary extensions towards long-term certainty.

**Source:** University of Birmingham, *Beyond Sanctuary: Building the Future of Ukrainians in the UK* (Policy Report, November 2026).
